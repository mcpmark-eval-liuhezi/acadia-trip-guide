# Acadia National Park — Fall Trip Guide (Oct 25–31)

**Official park page:** https://www.nps.gov/acad/index.htm

## 1. Park Overview

| | |
|---|---|
| **State** | Maine (ME) |
| **Designation** | National Park |
| **Park code** | `acad` |
| **Mailing address** | 25 Visitor Center Road, Hulls Cove Visitor Center, Bar Harbor, ME 04609 |
| **Contact** | 207-288-3338 · acadia_information@nps.gov |

**From the official NPS description:**

> Acadia National Park protects the natural beauty of the highest rocky headlands along the Atlantic coastline of the United States, an abundance of habitats, and a rich cultural heritage. At 4 million visits a year, it's one of the top 10 most-visited national parks in the United States. Visitors enjoy 27 miles of historic motor roads, 158 miles of hiking trails, and 45 miles of carriage roads.

**Key stats and facts for our group:**

- **Getting there:** From Boston, take I-95 north to Augusta, ME, then Route 3 east to Ellsworth and on to Mount Desert Island (alternate: I-95 to Bangor, then I-395 to US-1A to Ellsworth, then Route 3).
- **Park itself is open year-round, 24/7.**
- **Weather (NPS):** Fall temperatures range 30–70°F (−1 to 21°C). First frost is typically mid-October; first snowfall usually begins in November. Pack serious cold-weather layers for late October.
- **Entrance fees:** Private vehicle $35 (valid 7 days) · Motorcycle $30 · Per person (walk/bike) $20 · Acadia Annual Pass $70. Note: there's also a $6 timed-entry vehicle reservation fee for certain high-demand locations (e.g., Cadillac Mountain), which are separate from the entrance fee and do not permit re-entry.
- **Highlights for hikers:** 158 miles of hiking trails, 45 miles of carriage roads, granite headlands, mountains, lakes, night sky/stargazing, birdwatching, historic motor roads. Relevant activities listed by NPS include hiking (front-country), stargazing, birdwatching, paddling, biking, guided boat/bus tours, and picnicking.

## 4. Campground Situation

Acadia has **four campgrounds**, all reservation-only via Recreation.gov:

| Campground | Location | Sites | Season / Notes | Fees (per night) |
|------------|----------|-------|----------------|------------------|
| **Blackwoods** | East side of Mount Desert Island, ~5 mi south of Bar Harbor on Route 3 | 281 (221 tent-only, 60 RV, 4 group) | Seasonal, usually **early May to mid-October** — likely **closed for our late-October dates**. Wooded sites within a 10-minute walk of the ocean. Flush toilets, running water, dump station. No showers. | Tent $30 · RV $30 · Group tent area $60 |
| **Seawall** | West side of Mount Desert Island, ~4 mi south of Southwest Harbor (~18 mi from Bar Harbor) | — | Seasonal, typically **Wednesday before Memorial Day to Columbus Day** — likely **closed for our dates**. All sites a short walk from the coastline. | Standard $30 · RV $30 · Group tent area $60 |
| **Schoodic Woods** | Schoodic Peninsula, 1.5 mi southeast of Winter Harbor (~60–70 min from Bar Harbor) | 89 (78 with electric, 41 RV, 13 tent, 2 group, 9 walk/boat-in) | Seasonal, **Wednesday before Memorial Day until Columbus Day** — likely **closed for our dates**. Newest campground in the park. | Hike-in $22 · Tent w/ electric $30 · Standard electric $30 · RV electric+water $40 · RV electric $36 · Group $60 |
| **Duck Harbor** | Isle au Haut (rugged island off Stonington, ME) | 5 lean-to shelters | Reservations released April 1 for the entire season; reachable only by mailboat. Max 3 nights, 6 people per site, no pets, no generators. | $20 per shelter |

**⚠️ The big caveat:** all three of the park's large campgrounds (Blackwoods, Seawall, Schoodic Woods) operate on May-through-Columbus-Day (or mid-October) seasons. **Our Oct 25–31 window falls after their usual closing dates, so in-park camping is likely not available.** Since it's an island town, the group should plan on lodging in Bar Harbor, Southwest Harbor, or Northeast Harbor instead — and confirm final season-closing dates directly with the park (207-288-3338) before booking anything.

**Booking rules (for reference if the season extends):** 90% of sites released 6 months in advance on the first of each month at 10 a.m. EST; remaining 10% released 14 days ahead of arrival on a rolling basis. All reservations via Recreation.gov — the campground office cannot take reservations in person.

## 5. Arrival-Day Visitor Center

**Our pick: Hulls Cove Visitor Center** 🎯

- **Why:** It's Acadia's main visitor contact station and transportation hub, close to Bar Harbor off Route 3, adjacent to an entrance to the iconic 27-mile Park Loop Road, and a key stop for several Island Explorer (fare-free) bus routes. It's the classic first stop for visitors arriving on Mount Desert Island.
- **Address:** 25 Visitor Center Road, Bar Harbor, ME 04609
- **Hours:** Daily 8:30 a.m.–4:30 p.m. (season runs from the first week of May through the **end of October** — so it should still be open during our trip week, but note that after the end of October, visitor information moves to the Bar Harbor Chamber of Commerce at 2 Cottage St.)
- **Access notes:** visitors must climb 52 steps from the parking lot to the main entrance, but there's a separate accessible entrance.
- **Parking area tip:** the volunteer drop-in stewardship events meet at the Hulls Cove Storage Facility near the visitor center — if anyone signs up for Oct 27, 29, or 31, they'd already know the area.

**Other visitor facilities (for context):**

- **Sieur de Monts Nature Center** — visitor services, hiking info, interactive exhibits; trailhead for many hikes in the Sieur de Monts area. Open 9 a.m.–5 p.m. **June 14 through Oct 12, 2026 — closed by our dates.** (It's the meeting point for the Wetland Revival walks, though those occur outside.)
- **Acadia Gateway Center** — intermodal transit/welcome center on Route 3 in Trenton, 3 miles north of Mount Desert Island; Island Explorer bus stop, day-use parking, tourism info, store, restrooms. Open 8 a.m.–6 p.m. **May 20–Oct 12, 2026 — closed by our dates.**
- **Islesford Historical Museum** — boat-access-only museum on Little Cranberry Island; **closed for the season Sept 19, 2026.**

So Hulls Cove is really the only full-service visitor stop still operating during our week — another reason it's the arrival-day pick.

### Overview

| | |
|---|---|
| **State** | Virginia (VA) |
| **Designation** | National Park |
| **Address** | 3655 U.S. Highway 211 East, Luray, VA 22835 (administrative offices) |

**From the official NPS description:**

> Just 75 miles from the bustle of Washington, D.C., Shenandoah National Park is a land bursting with cascading waterfalls, spectacular vistas, fields of wildflowers, and quiet wooded hollows. With over 200,000 acres of protected lands that are haven to deer, songbirds, and black bear, there's so much to explore... and your journey begins right here!

- Four entrances: Front Royal (north), Thornton Gap, Swift Run Gap, and Rockfish Gap (south; also the northern end of the Blue Ridge Parkway). Park stretches 105 miles along Skyline Drive.
- **Weather:** four distinct seasons; fall brings cooler temperatures and spectacular foliage. The mountain is usually **10°F cooler than the valley below** — dress in layers and check the forecast.
- **Fees:** Private vehicle $30 (7 days) · Motorcycle $25 · Per person $15 · Annual pass $55. Fully **cashless** at fee stations since July 1, 2025.

## Summary & Action Items

1. **Arrival day:** stop at **Hulls Cove Visitor Center** (8:30 a.m.–4:30 p.m.) — grab maps, check trail conditions, and get the Park Loop Road lay of the land. It's the only visitor facility still open in late October.
2. **Campgrounds:** Acadia's three large campgrounds typically close by Columbus Day/mid-October — **in-park camping is likely unavailable Oct 25–31; book lodging in Bar Harbor or the other harbor towns instead**, and verify current closing dates with the park before committing.
3. **Events:** Ranger walks (Wetland Revival, Oct 28 & 30) and stewardship volunteering (Oct 27, 29, 31) are the events during our week — all weather-permitting and no reservations needed.
4. **Closures to route around:** Ocean Path's southern section, Beech Mountain Loop's east section (use the South/West Ridge alternates), and Schoodic Mountain Road (off-island).
5. **Weather:** plan for 30–70°F with possible frost; first snowfall can begin as early as November. Bring layers, traction, and rain gear.
6. **Plan B:** Shenandoah stays fully open through late October (Big Meadows through Nov 29) and has its fall foliage peak — a solid fallback if Maine weather kills the trip.
